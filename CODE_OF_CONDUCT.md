# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community. Be respectful, assume good intent, give actionable feedback, and focus discussion on the work.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and sustained disruption. Project maintainers may remove content or participation that violates these expectations.

Report conduct concerns privately to the project owner through GitHub. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
